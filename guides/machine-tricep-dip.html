Machine Tricep Dip

TRICEPSALL HEADS / HEAVYMACHINE

Watch it done

▶ Watch real form videos

What it trains

Triceps — All heads / heavy.

How to do it

  1. Grip the bars, press to a locked start, and lean your torso slightly forward.
  2. Lower under control until your upper arms are about parallel to the floor.
  3. Keep the elbows tracking back, not flared wide.
  4. Press back up, finishing just short of a hard lockout.
  5. More forward lean hits chest; staying upright shifts it to triceps.

Form checkpoints

  • Dropping too deep and straining the front of the shoulder.
  • Shrugging the shoulders up toward the ears — keep them pressed down.
  • Kipping or swinging the legs for momentum.

For your goals

Work in 6–10 reps heavy. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing.
