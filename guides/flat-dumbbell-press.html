Flat Dumbbell Press

CHESTMID / LOWER (STERNAL)DUMBBELL

Watch it done

▶ Watch real form videos

What it trains

Chest — Mid / lower (sternal). The bulk of the muscle; grows from any horizontal pressing. Training rule for this region: Flat pressing and dips.

How to do it

  1. Lie back with shoulder blades pulled together and feet flat on the floor.
  2. Start with the weight over mid-chest, wrists stacked over elbows.
  3. Lower slowly until you feel a full stretch across the chest.
  4. Drive back up in a slight arc, squeezing the pecs at the top without locking out hard.
  5. Keep your lower back lightly arched and your butt on the bench.

Form checkpoints

  • Cutting the range short — the stretched bottom half grows the most muscle.
  • Turning it into a shoulder movement by letting the shoulders roll forward.
  • Pressing to full hard lockout and resting there — it dumps tension.

For your goals

Work in 6–10 reps heavy. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing.
