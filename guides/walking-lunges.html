Walking Lunges

QUADSVASTUS LATERALIS (OUTER SWEEP)DUMBBELL

Watch it done

▶ Watch real form videos

What it trains

Quads — Vastus lateralis (outer sweep). The outer sweep — the visual one. Training rule for this region: Squats, leg press, lunges.

How to do it

  1. Take a stance long enough that your front shin stays near vertical at the bottom.
  2. Lower straight down until the back knee nearly touches the floor.
  3. Keep your torso tall and your weight through the front heel.
  4. Drive up through the front leg to stand.
  5. Finish all reps on one leg before switching.

Form checkpoints

  • Letting the front knee dive far past the toes with the heel lifting.
  • Pushing off the back leg instead of driving with the front.
  • Wobbling — slow down and own the balance.

For your goals

Work in 6–10 reps heavy. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing. Remember: the same lift at EOS and The Rec tracks separately — log each gym’s numbers as their own.
