Straight-Arm Pulldown

BACKLATS — PRIORITY (WIDTH)CABLE

Watch it done

▶ Watch real form videos

What it trains

Back — Lats — PRIORITY (width). Width and the V-taper — same waist-shrinking trick as side delts. Training rule for this region: Pull-ups and pulldowns.

How to do it

  1. Grip just outside shoulder width and start from a dead hang or fully stretched arms.
  2. Lead by driving the elbows down toward your back pockets.
  3. Pull until the bar reaches your upper chest (or your chin clears the bar), chest up the whole way.
  4. Squeeze the lats at the bottom of the pull for a beat.
  5. Return all the way to the stretch — long lats, full range, every rep.

Form checkpoints

  • Leaning way back and rowing the weight with momentum.
  • Cutting the top stretch short — that's where the lats grow.
  • Letting the shoulders shrug up into your ears at the stretch.

For your goals

Work in 6–10 reps heavy. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing. Remember: the same lift at EOS and The Rec tracks separately — log each gym’s numbers as their own.
