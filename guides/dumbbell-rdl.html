Dumbbell RDL

HAMSTRINGSHIP EXTENSION — PRIORITYDUMBBELL

What it trains

Hamstrings — Hip extension — PRIORITY. Loads the hamstring in a deep stretch — elite for growth, and best insurance against running injuries. Training rule for this region: Romanian deadlifts.

How to do it

  1. Start standing tall, bar or dumbbells against your thighs.
  2. Push your hips straight back with a soft knee bend — this is a hinge, not a squat.
  3. Lower along your legs until you feel a deep hamstring stretch (usually mid-shin).
  4. Keep the back flat and the weight close the entire way.
  5. Drive the hips forward to stand, squeezing the glutes at the top.

Form checkpoints

  • Rounding the lower back to reach lower — depth comes from hamstring flexibility only.
  • Bending the knees so much it becomes a squat.
  • Letting the bar drift away from the legs.

For your goals

Work in 10–15 reps controlled. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing.
