Wrist Extensions

What it trains

Forearms — Wrist flexors / extensors + grip. Underside and top-side thickness, plus grip. Training rule for this region: Wrist curls, farmer carries, dead hangs.

Why it’s in your plan

★ #5 research pick — Extensors sit on the visible top/outer forearm next to the brachioradialis; recover fast, tolerate 5-10 sets/wk.

How to do it

  1. Set up so the working muscle — not momentum — moves the weight.
  2. Start every rep from the fully stretched position.
  3. Move through the full range under control, 1-2 seconds up.
  4. Squeeze the target muscle hard at the peak for a beat.
  5. Lower slowly, 2-3 seconds, resisting the whole way.

Form checkpoints

  • Using momentum or other muscles to move the load.
  • Cutting the range of motion short on either end.
  • Choosing a weight you can't control on the negative.

For your goals

Work in 10–15 reps controlled. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing. Remember: the same lift at EOS and The Rec tracks separately — log each gym’s numbers as their own.
