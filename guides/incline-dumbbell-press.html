Incline Dumbbell Press

CHESTUPPER (CLAVICULAR) — PRIORITYDUMBBELL★ #3 RESEARCH PICK

▶ Watch real form videos

What it trains

Chest — Upper (clavicular) — PRIORITY.

Why it’s in your plan

★ #3 research pick — Deeper bottom stretch than barbell — stretch-mediated growth advantage, upper-pec bias.

How to do it

  1. Set the bench to a 30-45° incline — low enough that your front delts don't take over.
  2. Plant your feet, pull your shoulder blades back and down, and keep them pinned to the bench.
  3. Lower the weight under control to the upper chest, just below the collarbone.
  4. Press up and slightly back, stopping just short of locking the elbows to keep tension on the pec.
  5. Keep your wrists stacked over your elbows the whole rep.

Form checkpoints

  • Setting the incline too steep — past ~45° it becomes a shoulder press.
  • Bouncing the weight off your chest instead of controlling the stretch.
  • Flaring the elbows straight out — keep them ~45-60° from your torso.
  • Losing the shoulder-blade pinch at the bottom of the rep.

For your goals

Work in 6–10 reps heavy. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing. Remember: the same lift at EOS and The Rec tracks separately — log each gym’s numbers as their own.
