Chest-Supported Row

BACKMID-BACK (RHOMBOIDS, MID TRAPS)MACHINE/DB★ #3 RESEARCH PICK

What it trains

Back — Mid-back (rhomboids, mid traps). Thickness and posture. Training rule for this region: Rows, with a squeeze at the end.

Why it’s in your plan

★ #3 research pick — Nippard's #1 back exercise: stability removes low-back limits; full protract-retract drives trap/rhomboid growth.

How to do it

  1. Set up with a tall chest — on chest-supported versions, keep your sternum glued to the pad.
  2. Reach forward to a full lat stretch without letting the shoulders roll far forward.
  3. Pull the handle to your lower ribs, driving the elbows back and down.
  4. Squeeze the shoulder blades together for a beat at the back.
  5. Return slowly, letting the arms fully lengthen each rep.

Form checkpoints

  • Yanking with the lower back instead of pulling with the lats.
  • Shrugging the shoulders up during the pull.
  • Short-stroking the stretch — full reach forward every rep.

For your goals

Work in 6–10 reps heavy. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing.
