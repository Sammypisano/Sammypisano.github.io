Dumbbell Shrug

BACKUPPER TRAPS (COVERED BY ROWS)DUMBBELL

Watch it done

▶ Watch real form videos

What it trains

Back — Upper traps (covered by rows). The neck-to-shoulder slope. Covered by rows and deadlifts at this stage. Training rule for this region: Covered by rows and deadlifts.

How to do it

  1. Hold the weight with arms straight and shoulders relaxed down.
  2. Shrug straight up toward your ears as high as they'll go.
  3. Squeeze the traps hard for a second at the top.
  4. Lower slowly to a full stretch at the bottom.

Form checkpoints

  • Rolling the shoulders in circles — straight up and down only.
  • Bending the elbows and turning it into a row.
  • Cutting the stretch short at the bottom.

For your goals

Work in 10–15 reps controlled. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing.
