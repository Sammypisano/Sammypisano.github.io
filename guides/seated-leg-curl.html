Seated Leg Curl

HAMSTRINGSKNEE FLEXION (SEATED > LYING)MACHINE★ #1 RESEARCH PICK

What it trains

Hamstrings — Knee flexion (seated > lying). Seated leg curls beat lying — greater stretch on the muscle. Training rule for this region: Seated leg curls.

Why it’s in your plan

★ #1 research pick — Maeo 2021: ~50% more hamstring growth than lying curls (14% vs 9%) — hips flexed = biarticular hamstrings lengthened. Best runner-friendly pick.

How to do it

  1. Start from fully straight arms, elbows pinned to your sides.
  2. Curl the weight up in a smooth arc — no torso swing.
  3. Squeeze the biceps hard at the top for a beat.
  4. Lower slowly — 2-3 seconds down — to full extension.
  5. Keep the wrists neutral or slightly curled toward you.

Form checkpoints

  • Swinging the hips and lower back to start the rep.
  • Letting the elbows travel forward, turning it into a front raise.
  • Skipping full extension at the bottom.

For your goals

Work in 10–15 reps controlled. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing.
