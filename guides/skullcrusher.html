What it trains

Triceps — Long head — PRIORITY. Roughly half the arm's mass. Only fully stretches with the arm overhead or behind you — pushdowns miss it. Training rule for this region: Overhead extensions.

Why it’s in your plan

★ #2 research pick — Lower the bar behind the head — adds shoulder flexion and long-head stretch (Nippard S-tier).

How to do it

  1. Get the weight overhead (or over your face for lying versions) with elbows pointing forward, not out.
  2. Lower behind the head by bending only at the elbows until you feel a deep stretch in the long head.
  3. Keep the upper arms still and close to your ears.
  4. Extend back up to straight arms and squeeze.
  5. Use a controlled tempo — the stretched position is where the long head grows.

Form checkpoints

  • Elbows drifting wide — it takes tension off the triceps.
  • Turning it into a pullover by moving at the shoulder.
  • Loading too heavy to reach the deep stretch.

For your goals

Work in 10–15 reps controlled. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing.
