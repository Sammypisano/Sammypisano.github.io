Cable Lateral Raise

SHOULDERSSIDE DELT — TOP PRIORITYCABLE★ #1 RESEARCH PICK

Watch it done

▶ Watch real form videos

What it trains

Shoulders — Side delt — TOP PRIORITY. Widens the frame and creates the V-taper that makes the waist look smaller. Training rule for this region: Lateral raises — no momentum.

Why it’s in your plan

★ #1 research pick — Constant tension including the bottom/stretch where dumbbells give none; 2025 RCT shows equal growth to DBs — cables win on tension profile.

How to do it

  1. Stand tall holding the weights at your sides with a soft elbow bend.
  2. Raise out to the sides, leading with the elbows, until arms are about shoulder height.
  3. Tilt the pinky slightly up at the top, like pouring a drink — it keeps the side delt working.
  4. Lower slowly — take 2-3 seconds down.
  5. Stop each rep just before your traps start shrugging the weight up.

Form checkpoints

  • Swinging with momentum from the hips.
  • Shrugging the traps up to lift higher.
  • Going far above shoulder height — no extra side-delt work up there.

For your goals

Work in 10–15 reps controlled. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing.
