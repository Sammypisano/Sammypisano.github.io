Cross-Body Hammer Curl

What it trains

Forearms — Brachioradialis — THE VEIN MUSCLE. Thumb-side mass that gives the forearm its size — the cephalic vein runs right over it. Training rule for this region: Hammer and reverse curls, not wrist curls.

Why it’s in your plan

★ #3 research pick — Cross-body path further biases brachialis; rotate with strict hammers.

How to do it

  1. Hold the dumbbells with palms facing each other — they stay neutral the whole rep.
  2. Curl up with the elbows pinned to your sides.
  3. Squeeze at the top without letting the elbows drift forward.
  4. Lower slowly all the way to straight arms.
  5. The neutral grip loads the brachialis and brachioradialis — your focus muscles for arm thickness.

Form checkpoints

  • Swinging the torso to start the rep.
  • Half-lowering — full straight arms at the bottom.
  • Rotating the wrists mid-rep.

For your goals

Work in 10–15 reps controlled. Any set that hits 10+ reps means the weight goes up next time — the app bumps it by this lift’s own weight steps automatically. 8–9 reps = near max, 5–7 = medium-hard, 3–4 = hard pushing.
